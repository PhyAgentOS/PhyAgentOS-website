# Community platform logos

- `xiaohongshu.svg`, `bilibili.svg`, `youtube.svg`, `x.svg`, `wechat.svg`: [Simple Icons v14.0.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/14.0.0/icons), distributed under CC0. WeChat uses its brand green; social-card marks are displayed in white with CSS.
- `feishu.svg`: [Feishu app logo from its Flathub package](https://github.com/flathub/cn.feishu.Feishu/blob/master/cn.feishu.Feishu.svg). The white tile background is omitted and the view box tightened for the circular badge.

Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners. These assets are served locally so the community page does not depend on an external image CDN.
